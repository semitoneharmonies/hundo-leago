How to play

  1. Move the orange circle. Arrow keys aim sideways and up/down at your teammate, from ice level to above his waist. You can also click or drag directly on the rink. Keep adjusting your aim while holding Space.
  2. Cycle the power. Hold Space: power rises to full in 0.9 seconds, falls back down in 0.9 seconds, and repeats. Release to shoot at the current power.
  3. Trade speed for accuracy. Soft shots are slow, lobbed and accurate. Hard shots travel faster and flatter but can miss farther from your aim. The orange circle grows and shrinks with power: it marks the possible spread at your teammate.
  4. Beat three levels. Level 1: tip off the left post, right post and crossbar, each into the net. Level 2: clear all five targets. Level 3: clear all five past a goalie with a repeating movement pattern.
  5. Make your pucks count. Start with 20. Clearing Levels 1 and 2 adds another 20 to whatever you have left. Run out before clearing a level and the next run starts at Level 1.

Only blade or exposed-shaft tips count, including while your teammate crosses. Direct hits never clear an objective. In Level 1 the amber frame sections turn green when cleared; an iron hit that bounces out does not count.

Aim lower for lower targets and higher for upper targets. Pucks on the ice can slide into the grounded blade.

Change the target style any time using the menu at the top. Your shots and cleared targets stay the same. Only tipped hits break or knock down a target.

On touch screens: drag on the rink to aim, then hold and release Shoot. A second finger can adjust aim while you hold Shoot. Esc cancels a charge. P pauses. Leaving the window pauses the game.
