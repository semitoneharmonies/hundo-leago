03 · Fix the small misses

With the pump OFF, cross a small missed patch beside fresh blue ice to spread water onto it. Faded ice cannot supply water. Puddles spread farther and can be repaired with OFF; they also dry slowly.

04 · Check, then dump

Stop and press E to check the tank. Its meter remembers that reading; it does not update as you clean.

Turn the pump OFF, leave through the lower-left gate and drive into the dump bay. It empties automatically. A full tank stops cleaning, slows you down and spills snow to reclean.

Minimum dumps: level 1 → 1 · level 2 → 2 · level 3 → 3. These come from the snow load. Dumping early can mean extra trips. Once the ice is clean and puddle-free, the level ends—no final dump needed.

THE OPERATOR'S GUIDE

A clean pass is a finished pass.

Use FULL pump over snow, HALF on a partly overlapping pass, and OFF when crossing finished ice or stopping. Small overlaps are forgiven. Missed-spot view colours missed snow and dry ice bright red; pools stay dark blue. At 90% clean, red hints automatically glow more strongly and gently pulse outward to reveal small misses. Pressing the selected HALF or FULL again switches the pump OFF; 1 always means OFF. Standing still with the pump on creates a pool. Drive over it with the pump OFF for immediate repair, or let it slowly dry from the outside inward. Drying starts after 8 seconds without new water, then the edge shrinks every 3 seconds. More water delays drying.

Newly finished ice is bright blue for a short time. For 30 seconds, a pump-OFF pass can spread some of its water into nearby tiny missed patches while collecting their snow. Fresh ice fades to muted blue-grey as it sets. Set ice stays finished, but can no longer supply water. Larger untreated areas still need the pump. Near the end, tiny isolated specks surrounded by clean ice are tidied automatically when the tank has room.

Levels 1, 2 and 3 contain 1.4, 2.4 and 3.4 tankfuls of snow. One, two and three dumps are the minimums with efficient play. Dumping too early can require extra trips. There is no required dump count or final unloading trip. Finish each rink with no missed spots or pools to unlock the next. Press Enter or tap to continue; the timer pauses between levels and your next tank starts empty. A full tank stops collection and repairs, reduces speed to 40%, and drops snow behind you until you unload. Return to clean those spills afterward. Enter the striped bay to empty it automatically. The gauge changes only after a stopped inspection or unloading. The timer starts on first ice entry and continues during trips to the dump.

On mobile, double-tap DRIVE to cycle FULL → HALF → OFF → FULL. Hold the second tap to keep driving while your other thumb steers. The Drive button shows your pump level; the separate pump buttons still work.

The rink is 200 × 85 ft with a forgiving 10 ft cleaning path. The wider entrance is in the lower-left corner nearest the dump. Turn the pump OFF to drive out. Cleaning near the boards has a little extra reach. The rink fills the play area; the view pans toward the dump when you leave the ice. Steer while driving forward or reversing: the Zamboni follows smooth arcs and cannot pivot while stopped. Plan your turn before reaching the boards. Press V to highlight missed spots; red hints pulse automatically once the ice is 90% clean.
